Robin Hale: Thanks for making the time this morning. I have read what you sent over. To start, tell me what you do at Acme. ^p0001

Sam Rivera: I lead operations at Acme. Everything that happens on the shop floor comes through my team, from the first cut to the truck. ^p0002

Robin Hale: And for someone who has never seen the floor, what does Acme make? ^p0003

Sam Rivera: Acme makes shop fittings. Shelving, counters and display racks, mostly for small stores. ^p0004

Robin Hale: So what is the problem you want solved? ^p0005

Sam Rivera: Our orders live in three places. We tried bespoke systems twice to pull them together, and both times nobody kept them up. ^p0006

Robin Hale: Why did they fall over? Was it the tools, or the people around them? ^p0007

Sam Rivera: The people. Each one was built by a single person, and when that person left it went quiet. A system only one person understands is a system nobody owns. ^p0008

Robin Hale: That fits an idea from Imre Solano called the Paved Path. You watch where people already walk, and you pave that. You do not draw a new road and hope they take it. ^p0009

Sam Rivera: I like that. So where do my people already walk? ^p0010

Robin Hale: You tell me. When someone on the floor wants to know what is due this week, where do they look first? ^p0011

Sam Rivera: The shared order sheet. The floor team keeps it themselves, and they trust it more than anything the office gives them. ^p0012

Robin Hale: Then we start there. Call the project Acme Order Desk. One place for every order, built on the sheet the floor team already uses. ^p0013

Sam Rivera: Agreed. We build Acme Order Desk on the shared order sheet, not on a new system. That is decided. ^p0014

Robin Hale: Good. When do you want people using it? ^p0015

Sam Rivera: I want the first version in front of the floor team in January. The spring orders start to land in February, and I want it steady before then. ^p0016

Robin Hale: That is enough time if we keep it small. I will send you a proposal for Acme Order Desk by Friday. ^p0017

Sam Rivera: Send it to me and I will take it to the owners. They will ask what it costs to keep up, so put that in. ^p0018

Robin Hale: I will. Upkeep gets its own page. ^p0019

Sam Rivera: Then we are done. Thank you for coming in on a Tuesday. ^p0020
